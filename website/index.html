How the handoff works

One request keeps the firing traceable.

A digital infrastructure exists so you can see live kiln status and track your wares through our firing process.

  1. Dropoff requestTell us what you're bringing and when.
  2. Staff reviewStaff checks material, fit, timing, and handling.
  3. FiringYour work moves through low fire, medium fire, or custom.
  4. Ready noticeAfter cooling and a check, staff marks it ready.
  5. BillingAfter the fire is complete, billing is handled automatically.
  6. PickupCreate a pickup appointment, then come by after confirmation.
