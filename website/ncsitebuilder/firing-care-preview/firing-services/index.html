Firing Services

Drop off, fire fast, pick up without friction.

Monsoon Fire rents kiln space for ceramic artists who need fast turnarounds, flexible hours, and a clear handoff without joining a studio program.

Dropoff example

Simple pricing starts with fit.

At dropoff, the studio estimates kiln space from the shelf-size mat, then confirms the shelf count before loading. Accepted pieces move out of transport wrapping and into padded bread crates.

Normal order

3 plates, 2 mugs, 1 small bowl.

Usually starts as one half-shelf estimate when the pieces fit inside one mat footprint.

Wide or tall order

1 tall vase, 1 wide serving bowl.

Tall work can be laid on its side. If the vase uses one full mat and the wide bowl uses another, estimate two half-shelf spaces.

Low-friction handoffs

The service is built around getting work in and out.

01

Confirm the window

Send material notes, size, photos, and timing once. Staff replies with the best studio plan and a confirmed dropoff window.

02

Drop off quickly

Bring the pieces during the confirmed window. No studio membership, long tour, or mystery process is required.

03

Fire fast

Staff loads compatible work together and keeps the kiln schedule moving toward quick turnaround.

04

Pick up cleanly

The studio confirms when the work is cooled, checked, and staged so pickup can be one simple trip.

Flexible hours

Built around real studio schedules.

  • Dropoff and pickup windows can be coordinated around workdays, classes, and travel.
  • Morning or evening handoffs can be requested when the firing schedule allows.
  • Timing notes stay attached to the studio request instead of scattered across messages.

Fast turnarounds

Fast is the default goal.

The studio keeps speed as the normal service posture: quick routing, clear dropoff, staff loading, and pickup as soon as cooling and checks are done.
